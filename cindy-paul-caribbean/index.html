  4. 4Petit PitonThe smaller of the two UNESCO-listed volcanic spires.

Ideas from the sheet

  • Morning (8:30–12): guided 4x4 tour of the exclusion zone to see buried Plymouth — 'the Pompeii of the Caribbean'.
  • Lunch (12:15–1:30): local catch and rum at a beach grill in Little Bay.
  • Afternoon (2–4:30): Little Bay Beach, or a short boat trip to Rendezvous Bay's sea cliffs and caves.
  • Also on Seabourn's list: Centre Hills rainforest hike for endemic birds.

Montserrat

  1. 1Little BayShip calls 8am–6pm. Montserrat's small port and new town center.
  2. 2Rendezvous BayThe island's only white-sand beach — reached by boat or a steep hike.
  3. 3Montserrat Volcano ObservatoryViews across to the Soufrière Hills volcano and an explanatory film.
  4. 4Soufrière Hills VolcanoThe active volcano that reshaped the southern half of the island.
  5. 5Plymouth (buried capital)The former capital, buried by eruptions in 1995–97. Seen only on guided exclusion-zone tours.
